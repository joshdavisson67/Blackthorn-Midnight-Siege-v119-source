MENU / OPTIONS
Pause and resume

Press any button on a connected controller and it takes over, with no mouse lock needed. A key, click or tap hands control back. In menus, the D-pad or left stick moves between buttons, A presses, B goes back, the right stick scrolls, and left or right changes sliders and choices. Aiming slows your look speed, and it slows a little more while your sights are over an enemy. The pause menu can switch vibration off.

Click Enter the Estate to start and lock your mouse. Press Esc to release it and pause, then click Resume to lock it again. Use the pause menu’s Minimap Position toggle to choose top right or bottom left above health and shield; your choice is saved for future visits. Grenades have a short throwing animation; the gun lowers until the arm retracts.

02

Survive the night

Earn essence with hits, kills, and headshots. Clearing a wave earns a bonus. Each new wave brings faster, tougher enemies. Spend essence on weapons, ammo, and upgrades to keep up.

Enemies have solid bodies and can block doorways and stairs when they crowd together. Shoot a gap to get through. Press M to plan a route using the map of your current floor.

Make every bullet count

Headshots give a sharp audio cue, a gold hit mark and a stronger head reaction, even when the target survives. Toggle the small HEADSHOT label in the pause menu. Head, torso, arm and leg hits produce different flinches, and fatal shots can knock enemies back, twist them, drop a shoulder or buckle their knees.

Each weapon has its own kick and recovery. The Glock’s punch, the Raven’s lighter rapid fire and the heavier shotgun, LMG and Bushmaster feel different. A well-placed Glock headshot can briefly interrupt an ordinary zombie. Repeated powerful hits can stagger a brute. Stagger has a recovery window, so keep moving and firing.

Brief arrows around your sights point toward whoever hit you. Red means health damage; blue means your shield absorbed the whole hit. They follow your view as you turn.

Random world events · From wave 3

Each eligible wave has a 40% chance of a world event. Events never occur on consecutive waves, and only one can be active at once. Watch for the name and warning before it begins; the HUD shows its effects and remaining time. Pausing freezes events, and clearing the wave ends them. All four events last 30 seconds and award 20% more essence per kill while active, including headshots and every enemy type. Hit rewards and wave-clear bonuses keep their usual values.

The Shroud · 30 seconds

An eerie call warns of thick cemetery fog. Long-range visibility drops across the outdoor grounds, especially the cemetery. Interior rooms stay clear. Expect distant zombies to disappear into the mist. Kills award 20% more essence while the fog is active.

The Surge · 30 seconds

A supernatural pulse makes standard zombies move 20% faster and recover their attacks faster. All enemy types award 20% more kill essence during the event. Hit rewards and wave-clear bonuses stay the same. Dogs, Ashfang hounds, ferals and brutes keep their own movement speeds.

Grave Awakening · 30 seconds

Three old graves glow and tremble for four seconds before six extra zombies rise into the current wave. For the next 30 seconds, kills award 20% more essence. Defeat the reinforcements to finish the wave. Each drops a gold essence crystal worth 25 bonus essence; walk over it within 30 seconds to collect it.

Blackout · 30 seconds

The lamps gutter and hiss, then every light in the estate dies: lamps, candles, fires, windows and the moon. Only your flashlight cuts the dark, so keep it on (F) and watch for red eyes. Kills award 20% more essence until the lights stutter back.

Blood Rage

Every kill gives each living enemy within 6 meters a 15% chance to become enraged: double movement speed and 25% more damage. Watch for the red glow and bright red radar blips. It affects all enemy types, lasts until they die, and does not stack.

At the end of your run, you can enter your name on the shared leaderboard. Your score is all essence earned, including what you spent. The curse rank you chose and its title are recorded with your run. Scores persist as the game is updated.

03

Know the dead

Crawlers

Enough damage to the legs drops a walker or feral to the floor. Crawlers drag themselves along at under half speed and still bite if they reach you. Brutes and dogs keep their feet.

Zombie dogs

Low, fast quadrupeds with lunging bites. Both dog variants gain speed with the same wave progression as ordinary zombies.

Frenzied finale END OF WAVE 5

After the normal wave-5 enemies are cleared, 17 more arrive: 13 regular zombies and four brutes. The finale uses frenzy’s 40% faster movement and accelerated spawns. Defeat all 17 to finish wave 5; normal Blood Rage can still enrage nearby survivors.

Ashfang hounds FROM WAVE 6

Rare hounds with bone-plated skulls, dorsal spines, and glowing red eyes. They move 50% faster than the standard zombie baseline and have twice a normal dog’s health.

Feral stalkers FROM WAVE 7

Roughly one in eight enemies is a lean, pale zombie with glowing red eyes and claws. Ferals move at 3× normal wave speed and hit twice as hard. Enraged Ferals reach 6× speed and 2.5× damage.

From wave 11: enemies also spawn in upstairs rooms and can descend to pursue you.

04

Spend wisely

Glock 21 / .45 pistol · Starter weapon

Every new run begins with the Glock 21, a 13-round magazine, and 78 reserve rounds. Click once per shot and hold right mouse to aim through its open iron sights. Press R to reload and 1 to equip it. Supply crates, Max Ammo, Deadeye, Quickhands, and all three Soul Forge ranks work with the pistol. Buy the M4 inside the wave-7 shed for 4,500 essence; press 6 to equip it after purchase.

Buy weapons in the library and dining room. Upstairs, look for shotgun and SMG racks, plus the .450 Bushmaster in the east study. Supply crates in the courtyard, portrait gallery, infirmary, and observatory restock every owned weapon. Ammo purchases and Max Ammo leave your current magazine unchanged and preserve any reload already in progress. They include enough spare rounds to leave both magazine and reserves full after reloading.

AR-15 / .450 Bushmaster · 2,500 essence

Find the olive-stock rifle rack upstairs in the east study, marked BM on the estate map. It has a 15-round magazine, 90 reserve rounds, and twice the M4’s damage. Press 5 to equip it. Click once per shot; holding fire does not repeat. Hold right mouse for its clear 10× precision scope. Ammo refills and the Soul Forge work with this rifle too.

Grenade blast. Grenades deal 450 damage within the inner 75% of their 12-meter blast radius (up to 9 meters). The outer 25% deals half damage (225). Walls and solid floors block the blast. Stay clear of nearby explosions.

Soul Forge · 3 ranks per weapon

Find the iron anvil beneath a hovering violet soul stone on the first floor or against the back wall of the storm cellar. The cellar forge is reachable after the storm doors open at wave 10. Rank 1 costs 5,000 essence, Rank 2 costs 10,000, and Rank 3 costs 15,000. Ranks 1, 2, and 3 set your weapon damage to 2×, 4×, and 6× its base damage. Each rank still reduces reload time by 25% and multiplies magazine and reserve capacity by 1.25; these ammo and reload bonuses compound. Ammo capacities round up after multiplying the base capacity for your rank. Quickhands stacks with every rank. Each weapon tracks its own rank at both forges; switching weapons updates the next rank and price. Forging keeps your carried rounds, so reload or buy ammo to fill the increased capacity. All ranks reset with a new run.

Aegis armor · 2,500 essence. Buy a vest in the storm cellar or upstairs east study to gain a separate shield equal to your maximum health. Armor absorbs damage before health. Ironheart also scales its remaining protection.

Nano Shield Rank 1 · 5,000 essence. After buying Aegis, both original armor stations offer Rank 1. Your shield regenerates at 5 shield per second after 5 seconds without a hit.

Nano Shield Rank 2 · 7,500 essence. Buying Rank 1 unlocks the gold Rank 2 emitter at those same stations in the storm cellar and upstairs east study. Press E at either station to upgrade: recharge now starts after 3 seconds without a hit and restores 10 shield per second.

Nano Shield Rank 3 · 20,000 essence. Buy Rank 2 to unlock the emitter with gold rays at both armor stands. Rank 3 restores 15 shield per second continuously, with no start delay. Taking hits no longer interrupts shield regeneration.

Each rank can be bought once per run. All ranks keep your current shield amount and capacity and recover even from zero shield, up to full. Hits restart the recharge delay for Ranks 1 and 2; Rank 3 keeps regenerating during combat. Recharging pauses with the game and stops when you die.

05

Power-ups

Walk over a dropped pickup to collect it. Recognize the effect by its icon.

Ammo box · Max Ammo

Restocks every owned weapon without loading its magazine. Reload to fill it; your reserves will still be full. Grants one grenade.

×2 · Double Damage

Double bullet damage for 20 seconds. Relic shrines sell it for 800 essence.

Grenade · Infinite Grenades

Unlimited grenades for 12 seconds, with a 0.5-second cooldown after each throw animation. Press G to throw. Relic shrines sell it for 900 essence.

Timed-bonus shrines are available on both floors.

06

Explore the estate

The second floor

Take the grand hall staircase to find all four perks, shotgun and SMG racks, ammunition, timed-bonus shrines, and an Aegis armor vest in the east study. The doors at the ends of both wing corridors open onto steel landings and exterior stairways to the grounds. Use them in either direction; zombies and Bella can follow. Remember: upstairs enemy spawns begin at wave 11.

Bella / Immortal Maltipoo · 670 essence

Find Bella’s kennel against the rear wall of the upstairs west bedchamber, marked B on the floor map. Approach the kennel and press E to free her once per run. Bella follows you through the estate and its stairs, and automatically bites living undead within 12 yards of you, including dogs, ferals and brutes. Walls and floors block bites. She has no health, cannot be hurt, and does not block your movement. Her kills earn regular body-kill rewards toward your essence and shared score. Pausing stops her movement and attacks; a new run returns her to her kennel.

The groundskeeper’s shed OPENS AT WAVE 7

Explore the north grounds beside the storm cellar to find a weathered timber shed. Its barred doors blow open automatically when wave 7 begins. A notification announces that a door has blown open somewhere. Approach the M4 on the rear workbench and press E to purchase it for 4,500 essence, once per run. It equips the M4 with a full magazine and reserve, keeping its Soul Forge ranks. Press 6 to equip it later; enemies can follow you inside.

The storm cellar OPENS AT WAVE 10

Find the twin storm doors on the north grounds, opposite the starting courtyard. Descend to the armory and press E to buy the Warden LMG for 5,000 essence. Select it with 4. It carries 100 rounds and 500 reserve and can be purchased once per run. Hold right mouse to aim through its clear 3× combat scope; release to return to the wider view. The Soul Forge stands against the back wall between the LMG and armor stations, facing into the room. Its three weapon ranks are shared with the first-floor forge.

Taking the LMG triggers a frenzy

Enemies move 40% faster, spawns speed up, and reinforcements arrive for the rest of your current wave.

The family cemetery MAUSOLEUM OPENS AFTER WAVE 20

Follow the east grounds through the iron gateway into the graveyard. The Blackthorn mausoleum opens automatically after you clear wave 20. Explore its stone chamber, but enemies can follow you inside. Something sleeps in the sarcophagus.

The Last Dawn / Nuke · 20,000 essence

Once the mausoleum opens, find the radiation-marked device against its side wall. Approach it inside the tomb and press E to activate it once per run. It destroys every living, currently spawned enemy across the entire estate, including hounds, ferals, and brutes. Each earns the normal body-kill bounty: 50 essence, or 110 for a brute, with The Surge’s extra 20% when active and your curse reward multiplier. Grave-born enemies also drop their gold essence. It also counts toward your kills and shared score. Queued enemies still spawn normally. Detonating it wakes Sir Blackthorn, even on an empty estate.

07

Sir Blackthorn & the curse ladder

Detonate The Last Dawn in the mausoleum after wave 20 and the sarcophagus lid slides away. Sir Blackthorn, the estate's first lord, climbs out. Get clear of the tomb while he rises. Regular spawns hold while he fights, and the run carries on as far as you can make it once he falls.

Grave lance

From range he raises an arm and hurls a cursed bolt along your line. Strafe out of its path; walls stop it. Below half health he throws three at once.

The Gravewarden

At three-quarters and two-fifths health he summons a Gravewarden: a slow, iron-clad tank with a tower shield. Shots into the shield do a quarter damage, so flank it or aim around it.

War cry

His scream drives every undead within 18 m into a red fury for 10 seconds: 35% faster and 30% harder-hitting. Thin his allies before he cries again.

Below 30% health he is enraged: faster, and quicker to throw and cry. Defeating him pays 5,000 essence (multiplied by your curse) and drops a Max Ammo.

Climb the curse. Pick your curse rank on the title screen before you enter, and it stays the same for the whole run. Defeat Sir Blackthorn at your highest unlocked rank to unlock the next. Unlocked ranks are remembered on this device.

Each rank adds 15% enemy speed, 25% newly earned essence and score, and 10% relative special enemy frequency. Speed and special frequency each cap at +100%, with the existing limit of 32 living enemies. Rank 3 begins adding 20% enemy health per further rank; rank 4 begins adding 15% enemies per wave per further rank. Purchase prices stay the same.

Titles: 0 Uncursed · 1 The Whispered · 2 The Marked · 3 The Forsaken · 4 The Damned · 5 Blackthorn’s Chosen · 6+ Endless Corruption. Pause to see your rank and modifiers. The shared leaderboard records each run's curse rank and title with its score.
